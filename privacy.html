Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Jarvis is a personal desktop app used by its owner on their own computer. This policy explains what it does with Google account data.

What Jarvis accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): messages in the inbox from the last 30 days, to find requests and deadlines.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): events from the day before to two weeks ahead, to show the day's fixed events.
  • Basic profile: the account's email address, to tell connected accounts apart.

Jarvis cannot send, delete or change email or calendar events.

Where the data is kept

Data is stored only on the owner's computer, in a local database. Sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows data protection. Nothing is uploaded to a Jarvis server; there is none.

How the data is used

Data is used only to show the owner their own requests, deadlines and calendar. The owner may choose to let their own AI assistant (Claude, under the owner's account) read this data on their computer to sort email and write a daily summary. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Disconnecting an account in Jarvis deletes its stored sign-in tokens. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Jarvis data folder removes all stored data.
